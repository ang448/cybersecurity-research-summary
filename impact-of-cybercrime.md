# Impact of Cybercrime

Different types of cybercrime may cause individuals, businesses, public services and overall society issues depending on what information has been impacted.

## Financial cost

Victims may lose money due to fraud, theft, internal services disruption, or replacing these impacted services/equipment.

## Leak of personal information

People will be plagued by unwanted communications, personal information being used with incorrect intent and further attempts to defraud you. In addition organisations may suffer from legal and regulatory issues.

## Service disruption

Cybercrimes can disrupt services within businesses, such as, a hospital may experience disruptions to their admin/clinical services.

## Loss of reputation

Any customers, staff or partners in an organisation may lose confidence.

## Emotionally impacting

Those that experienced cybercrime may become stressed, anxious and lose confidence in online services.

## How to reduce Cybercrime impacts

Organisations can prevent cybercrime by regularly backing up data, installing security patches, restricting access, providing staff with training and by monitoring the systems. Individuals can avoid cybercrime using multi-factor auth, unique passwords and have good online practices.

## Summary

Cybercrime isn’t just an IT issue. It can cause financial, operational, personal and social issues so it is important to avoid/prevent and be prepared.
